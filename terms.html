You must be at least 16 years old. Alergia is not meant for anyone younger.

3. What the app does

Alergia lets you save your allergens, scan the barcode of a packaged food and see a safety status (No Allergens Found, Use Caution, Not Safe for You or Safety Unknown), see a daily pollen and weather risk level, and ask an AI assistant allergy questions. The app is free, has no ads and has no in-app purchases.

4. Not medical advice

Alergia is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Safety statuses, risk levels, pollen and weather information and the AI assistant's answers are for information only and may be wrong or incomplete. "No Allergens Found" only means that Alergia did not find your allergen in the product data, which can be incomplete or wrong, and it does not guarantee that a product is free of your allergen. Always read the label on the product and ask a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns. In case of a severe allergic reaction (for example difficulty breathing, swelling or fainting), seek emergency medical help immediately.

5. Data from other sources

Product names, ingredients and allergen tags come from Open Food Facts, a community database (ODbL licence); they can be missing, outdated or mistaken. Weather and pollen data come from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) and are available only in some regions and seasons. The AI assistant runs on Groq and reaches it through a small relay hosted on Cloudflare. We do not control these services and cannot promise that they are accurate or always available.
